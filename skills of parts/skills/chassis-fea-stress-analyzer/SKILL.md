# Chassis FEA Stress Analyzer

A domain skill for chassis structural engineering and FEA load path analysis.

## Torsional Rigidity Targets
- **Steel Ladder Frame**: $8 - 12\text{ kNm/deg}$
- **Pressed Steel Unibody**: $20 - 30\text{ kNm/deg}$
- **Aluminum Spaceframe**: $32 - 42\text{ kNm/deg}$
- **Carbon Monocoque Tub**: $45 - 65+\text{ kNm/deg}$
